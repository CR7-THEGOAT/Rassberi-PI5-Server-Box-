# REV27 — no small lid circle and no rear Ethernet-board box

This is a separate complete version. It keeps the REV26 body size, removable slide lid, Raspberry Pi side ports, five storage bays, VCOM adapter clearance, and the latest solid-front U-handle trays.

## Removed in REV27

- The small centred 1-inch circle in the lid is filled; the lid is solid there.
- The separate rear five-port Ethernet-board compartment is removed.
- Its internal support, rear RJ45 openings, 12 V round power opening, and the Ethernet-board air/capacitor opening are all closed, leaving a continuous rear wall.

## Still present

- The normal Raspberry Pi Ethernet plus four USB-A openings on the right side.
- The Pi USB-C and two Micro-HDMI openings.
- The solid-front trays with attached round U-pull handles.

## Use

`Server_Box_REV27_NO_CIRCLE_NO_REAR_ETHERNET_ASSEMBLY.stl` is one complete assembled-view file. It has seven disconnected components inside it, so do not print it as one piece.

Print `base_REV27.stl` once, `top_REV27_slide_lid.stl` once, and `tray_REV27_U_Pull.stl` five times.
